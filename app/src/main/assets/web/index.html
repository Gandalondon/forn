How it works

Go about your day

Forn notices the places you pass: near, under or over. At 9pm it tells you the best of them. Up to five, fewer on a quiet day.

Dust

Every place you pass leaves dust on that day's ring: history you haven't uncovered yet. Lots of dust means you walked through lots of history.

Balls

Each night Forn uncovers up to five places, and they become black balls. Tap one to read it again. Pass the dusty places again to uncover more. One ring for each day, today in the middle.

What it needs

Your location, all the time, so it knows what you passed with your phone in your pocket. Notifications, for 9pm.

Tell me more

Tap a ball, then Tell me more. It hands a question about that place to the app you pick: your AI, a search or your notes.

Privacy

No track of where you went. It only notes the places you passed and when. Nothing leaves your phone, unless you share a place with Tell me more.
