App name (30): Forn: London History Near You

Short description (80):
London's history in the places you pass every day, told at 9pm.

Full description:
You pass history every day. Forn notices it for you.

Go about your day as normal. Forn quietly notices the old places you pass: what's under the street, the building on the corner, the common you walk the dog on. At 9pm it tells you the best of them. Up to five, fewer on a quiet day.

Every day leaves a ring
Each day becomes a ring, today in the middle, older days rippling out. The places Forn tells you about become black balls, placed by the time you passed them. Tap one to read it again. Everything else you passed stays as dust: history you haven't uncovered yet. Pass by again and you might uncover more.

No pings while you're out
Nothing buzzes in your pocket. One moment at 9pm, then it leaves you alone.

Nothing leaves your phone
Forn needs your location all the time, so it knows what you passed with your phone in your pocket. It doesn't keep a track of where you went, only which places you passed and when. No account, no ads. The only thing that ever leaves is what you choose to send with Tell me more.

Tell me more
Want to dig deeper? Tap Tell me more and Forn hands a question about that place to the app you pick: your AI, a search or your notes.

London only, for now
Facts are written from Historic England and local history sources. Other cities later.
