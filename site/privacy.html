Privacy

Who we are

Forn is made by Tony Goff-Yu and run by Goff & Associates Limited, which is responsible for your personal information.

On your phone

The places you passed, and when, stay on your phone. No account, no cookies, no analytics, no adverts.

Your location

Forn uses your phone's location, including when the app is closed, so it can notice when you pass an old place. That happens on your phone, through Android's location service (Google Play services, which works under Google's own privacy policy). Your location's never sent to us or to anyone else.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose Tell me more. That shares the one place you're reading about with the app you pick.

Other services

This site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard logs such as IP address and time, possibly outside the UK under standard safeguards. Buying Forn goes through Google Play, under Google's policy. We never see your card details.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Forn, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Nothing's kept anywhere else.

Feedback

If you email us at hello@getforn.app, we use your message and address only to reply.
