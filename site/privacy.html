Privacy

Forn keeps everything on your phone: the places you passed and when. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Your location

Forn uses your phone's location, including when the app is closed, so it can notice when you pass an old place. That happens on your phone, through Android's location service (Google Play services, which works under Google's own privacy policy). Your location isn't sent to us or to anyone else.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose Tell me more. That shares the one place you're reading about with the app you pick.

Deleting

Uninstall Forn and everything it kept goes with it.

This site

No cookies and no tracking. If you email us, through Try it early or Tell me, we only use your email to reply.
